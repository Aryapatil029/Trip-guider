Pabbar valley

Pabbar Valley is an enchanting, offbeat valley located roughly 80 to 100 kilometres east of Shimla in Himachal Pradesh, India. Carved by the pristine Pabbar River, this region—frequently referred to as the Rohru Valley—is highly celebrated by adventurers.

Kedarnath

Kedarnath is located in the Rudraprayag district of Uttarakhand at an altitude of 3,584 metres, this ancient temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva. It stands as one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas in India and forms a key destination of the Char Dham Yatra

Shillong

Shillong is a scenic hill station and the capital city of Meghalaya, India, widely celebrated as the "Scotland of the East" due to its rolling pine-covered hills and cool climate. Situated at an average altitude of 1,520 metres above sea level in the East Khasi Hills district, it serves as the cultural, educational, and commercial hub of the state.

Ladakh

Ladakh, established as a Union Territory of India on October 31, 2019, is a high-altitude, sparsely populated region known as the "Land of High Passes". Situated between the Kunlun and Himalayan mountains, it features a cold desert climate, Tibetan Buddhist culture, and major cities Leh and Kargil.

Shimla and Manali

Shimla and Manali are premier hill stations in Himachal Pradesh, offering contrasting experiences: Shimla, the state capital and former British summer capital, features colonial charm, the Ridge, and bustling Mall Road at $\sim$2,200m. Manali, nestled in the higher Kullu Valley at $\sim$2,050m, is a hub for adventure sports, snow-capped peaks, and backpacker culture

Dalhousie

Dalhousie is a serene, colonial-era hill station in Himachal Pradesh, India, established in 1854 and named after Lord Dalhousie. Located on five, 1,970-meter-high hills, it features Victorian-style churches, pine-clad valleys, and panoramic Himalayan views. Popular for trekking, nature walks, and nearby spots like Khajjiar and Kalatop.

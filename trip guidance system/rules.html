Travel Instructions

Important: Please read all rules carefully before booking your trip.
  1. Carry a valid government ID proof during the journey.
  2. Arrive at the departure location at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time.
  3. Follow all local laws, regulations, and safety instructions.
  4. Keep your valuables secure. The company is not responsible for lost items.
  5. Respect fellow travelers, guides, and local communities.
  6. Do not litter. Help maintain cleanliness at tourist destinations.
  7. Consumption of illegal substances is strictly prohibited.
  8. Follow environmental conservation guidelines during nature tours.
  9. Any damage caused to hotel or transport property will be charged to the traveler.
  10. In case of emergency, immediately contact the trip coordinator or local authorities.

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation 7 days before trip: 80% refund.
  • Cancellation 3-7 days before trip: 50% refund.

Matheran

Matheran is a budget-friendly hill station in Maharashtra known for its pleasant weather, scenic viewpoints, and vehicle-free environment. Visitors can enjoy toy train rides, nature walks, and beautiful sunsets while keeping travel expenses relatively low.

Lonavala & Khandala

Lonavala and Khandala are popular weekend destinations located near Mumbai and Pune. They offer affordable accommodations, waterfalls, caves, and viewpoints, making them ideal for families seeking a refreshing and economical getaway.

Panchgani

Panchgani is a peaceful hill station famous for its strawberry farms, cool climate, and scenic valleys. It provides comfortable lodging options and affordable sightseeing attractions such as Table Land and Sydney Point.

Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar is a well-known hill station offering beautiful viewpoints, lush greenery, and fresh strawberries. With numerous budget hotels and local eateries, it is an excellent destination for travelers looking for value and comfort.

Saputara

Saputara, Gujarat's only hill station, is known for its lake, gardens, ropeway, and pleasant climate. The destination offers affordable accommodation and sightseeing opportunities, making it suitable for travelers on a moderate budget.

Mount Abu

Mount Abu is a scenic hill station in Rajasthan featuring Nakki Lake, Dilwara Temples, and Sunset Point. The town offers a range of budget and mid-range hotels, making it a cost-effective choice for a relaxing vacation.
